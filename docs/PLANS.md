# Velorise's Staves & Elements — Public Roadmap

> High-level overview of upcoming features and improvements. For resolved issues and shipped updates, see [FIXED.md](FIXED.md).

## Upcoming Staves & Combat Reworks
- **Vortex Vanquisher (Geo Staff):** Summon resonant Geo Steles and form a scaling Jade Shield with nearby resistance shred.
- **Splendor of Tranquil Waters (Hydro Staff):** Dual-stance Ousia/Pneuma staff with Salon Members, AoE healing, and water-walking.
- **Tome of the Eternal Flow (Hydro Rework):** Continuous channeled Hydro beam with Sourcewater Droplet pickup and movement while casting.
- **Mistsplitter Reforged & Elemental Swords:** Expand element-imbued sword variants and elemental infusion mechanics.

## Mechanics, Balance & Quality of Life
- **Elemental Protection (Armor Enchantment):** New armor enchantment book to mitigate incoming elemental & reaction damage, with configurable armor durability rules.
- **Dedicated Elemental Damage Type:** Separate elemental and reaction damage from generic magic to establish unique combat identities and custom damage scaling.
- **Elemental Resonance Balance Tuning:** Fine-tune Pyro & Hydro resonances to prevent excessive damage double-dipping while boosting Hydro sustain.
- **Dynamic Co-Op Mob Scaling:** Proximity-based multi-player enemy health and damage scaling inspired by Genshin Co-Op mode.
- **Authentic EGT Combat Nuances:** Swirl-triggered Dendro Core detonations, Shatter blunt priority, and dynamic Burning fuel consumption.
- **Elite Elemental Shields:** Specialized reaction-depleted elemental shield layers for rare mutated mobs and bosses.
- **Dynamic Elemental Particle & Visual Polish:** Enhanced reaction feedback, elemental pet death formatting, and chromatic Omni styling.
- **New Advancements & Immersion:** Additional challenge advancements and progression milestones.
